# Simon AI Coach — Written Proposal

## The Problem

Productivity enthusiasts face a common challenge: they want to build better systems, follow through on their goals, and maintain consistent habits, but traditional coaching is expensive ($200-500/session), scheduling is a hassle, and generic productivity apps don't understand their unique context, values, and preferences.

Simon's audience — people who love great design and building systems — needs coaching that's:
- **Accessible**: Available 24/7, no scheduling required
- **Affordable**: Fraction of the cost of traditional coaching
- **Personal**: Understands their context and remembers their goals
- **Actionable**: Creates plans, schedules events, and tracks progress
- **Beautiful**: Clean, minimal design that respects their time

---

## The Solution

**Simon AI Coach** is a minimalist iOS app that brings personalized AI coaching to your pocket. It's designed for productivity enthusiasts who want guidance at the right moment, without complex setup or expensive subscriptions.

### Core Experience

1. **Browse or Create Coaches**: Choose from 12+ curated coaches (Focus, Planning, Creativity, Decision-Making, Wellness, Business, etc.) or create your own custom coach with AI-generated avatars, personalized specialties, and coaching styles.

2. **Chat Naturally**: Communicate via text, voice messages, or document uploads. Responses stream in real-time with optional voice-over using natural, human-like speech.

3. **Get Actionable Guidance**: Coaches don't just talk — they create structured plans with milestones, schedule calendar events and reminders, set up recurring check-ins, and remember your preferences and commitments.

4. **Quick Access**: Use "Moments" for instant guidance with pre-built templates or direct chat that automatically routes you to the best coach.

5. **Personalize Everything**: Choose your theme (Light/Dark/System), accent color (6 options), font style (System/Rounded/Serif), and text size (4 sizes). All preferences sync across devices via Firebase.

### What Makes It Different

**Minimalist Design**: No clutter, no complexity. Open the app, pick a coach, start chatting. Every interaction is fast and intuitive.

**Multi-Modal Input**: Text when you're at your desk, voice when you're on the go, documents when you need context-aware advice.

**Voice-Over Responses**: Enable voice-over and your coach speaks back to you with natural speech. It feels like having a real coach in your pocket.

**Intelligent Actions**: Coaches create plans with milestones and next actions, schedule calendar events with reminders, create iOS reminders with due dates, schedule push notifications for check-ins, and remember your values, goals, and constraints.

**Deep Personalization**: Make the app yours with themes, colors, fonts, and text sizes. All synced via Firebase across all your devices.

**Free to Start, Affordable to Upgrade**: 3 free messages per session to try any coach. Upgrade to Pro for unlimited messaging, custom coach publishing, and advanced features. Plans start at $3.99/week.
